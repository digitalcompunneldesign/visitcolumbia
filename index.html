04 — Signature events

Plan around the big ones

Columbia's calendar has a rhythm. Here's when the town comes out to celebrate — and how far ahead to plan.

1840swhen Mule Day began as “Breeder's Day,” a livestock show and mule market
3daysof Mule Day each spring, with the parade on Saturday
9monthsof First Fridays downtown, April through December
45misouth of Nashville — an easy day trip, a better weekend

Mark your calendar

Every April

Mule Day

April 1–3, 2027

Parade, square dance, mule-driving contest, crafts and Appalachian food and music.

Event details
Monthly · Apr–Dec

First Fridays

First Friday · 5–8 PM

Craft vendors, food trucks, live music on the Main Stage and a Game Zone for all ages.
